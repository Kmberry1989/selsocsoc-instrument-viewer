How the jam works in the game

In Selfie Social Society, the plaza jam spot sits just south of the festival string lights: 7 instruments arranged on an arc around a wooden jam mat (radius 3.4). Tapping an instrument in the world opens the jam panel. Each instrument is a track with 5 pentatonic pads — C, D, E, G, A — at that instrument's own octave (see the table below). The sequencer walks steps across the tracks, playing each track's voice at its step note. You can build a loop on the grid, and when it sounds right you press it to vinyl: the record is saved and can be played back on your home record player, through the same voices.
